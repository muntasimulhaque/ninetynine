Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy describes how the app The Ninety Nine Names of Allah (the “App”) handles your information.

Summary

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no internet access. Everything you do in the App stays on your device.

What data does the App collect?

None. The App does not collect any personal information, and it does not use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What data is stored on your device?

All content in the App, including the 99 Names and their meanings, is bundled with the App and stored locally on your device. Your progress, bookmarks, and settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Does the App access the internet?

No. The App has no internet permission and performs no network activity.

Children’s privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from children or anyone else.
